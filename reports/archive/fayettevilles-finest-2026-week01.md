## Weekly Recap

WE’RE SO BACK 😭🏈 Twelve teams walked into Week 1 convinced they drafted a contender. Six walked out undefeated. The other six are already reading waiver-wire articles at work like they’re studying for the BAR 📚💀 We had a 183-point tactical nuke ☢️ a 161-point beatdown, two games decided by fewer than seven points, and PARKANSAS putting up a score that looked like somebody forgot to finish setting the lineup. The 2026 campaigns have officially begun. 😈🔥

### I’m all JAGged up vs Someone Calls Me Daddy
Tucker didn’t win Week 1… he reenacted the Death Star test fire💀🌎 Kenneth Walker dropped 34.1, D’Andre Swift went for 32.4, Isaiah Likely exploded for 27.8, Trevor Lawrence added 26.1, and Pittsburgh’s defense casually threw in 19 because apparently 164 wasn’t disrespectful enough. 😭🔥 The insane part is Ryan’s team wasn’t even terrible: Gibbs 33.6, Olave 28.2, and Jaxson Dart scored 26.6. That trio would normally have you celebrating. Instead they looked like the Avengers arriving to fight Thanos after he already had all six Infinity Stones🫰☠️ JAGged up opens with 183 and immediately becomes everybody’s problem👀

### Im in RA vs King Deshaun CeeDees Nuts
David put up 130.82 with Derrick Henry bulldozed his way to 35.3 👑🚂 Hurts added 24.72, and Evan McPherson somehow dropped 19 points from the kicker spot like he thought he was Steph Curry 🏀🔥 Unfortunately, Gage assembled the freaking Sinister Six🕷️: Jeanty 32.7, Amon-Ra 28.7, Flowers 26, Javonte 24.2, and Parker Washington 19.3. 💣💣💣 Im in RA starts 1-0 with 161.94, while David gets the beautiful fantasy experience of scoring 130 and being told “better luck next week.” 🫠

### Author CGreer v Waddle Deez Nuts
CGreer apparently downloaded the Week 1 script before everyone else 🤖🔥 Bijan dropped 31.3, Montgomery scored 28.9, and Watson came flying off the top rope with 32.7🫪. Carson actually got a monster 35.66 from Josh Allen and 26.2 from JSN, but then Kyle Pitts contributed a majestic 0.0 🥚😭 Waddle Deez Nuts had enough star power to stay respectable, but CGreer had too many weapons firing at once 🔫

### Dirty Graham vs PARKANSAS
Dirty got 37.26 from Caleb Williams, 25.1 from Jonathan Taylor, 19.8 from Breece Hall, and 16.9 from Mike Evans while PARKANSAS responded with the fantasy equivalent of BLT on sourdough type of performance 🥪🥪💀 Chase Brown’s 18.8 was basically Parker standing alone waiting for help that never came. Burrow gave 14.16, Saquon 9, Dowdle 4.1, Henry 5.6, and the kicker chipped in one whole point 🥀 Dirty Graham cruises to 1-0 while PARKANSAS immediately gets assigned waiver wire homework📚😭

### NFL Youngboy is back vs Ladd To The Bone
Now THIS is actual Week 1 stress 😭🍿 Will had Lamar Jackson drop 24.96, McConkey add 19.2, and Hockenson chip in 13.6, but Connor answered with Justin Jefferson’s 31.2 and Trey McBride’s 24.5 carrying the roster like LeBron dragging the Cavs to the Finals. 🏀💀 Drake Maye gave Connor only 9.82 and Pickens managed 5.8, so this thing was basically held together with duct tape and prayer. 🙏 Ladd To The Bone stayed within one possession all night, but Youngboy survives 107.22-100.66. Not pretty. Not dominant. Still 1-0.

### Bentonville Football Team vs Ivy League Mean Machine
Ben Brown escaped Week 1 by 4.26 points in a matchup where neither roster exactly like a possible championship contender 😭 Bentonville got 20.3 from Bucky, 17.66 from Daniels and 15.5 from Williams, while Odom countered with Nico Collins’ 21.2 and solid production from CMC and Etienne. Then the Browns defense dropped -1.0 🚬🚬🚬🚬 Terry McLaurin’s 3.4 didn’t exactly save Bentonville either, but Ben survives the mud fight 102.36-98.10, and sometimes fantasy football is art. Sometimes it’s two raccoons fighting behind a Chili’s🦝😭
