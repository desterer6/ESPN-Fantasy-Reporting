## Important archival cautions
- Historical team names are preserved as reported at the time. A name may have changed before later weeks; this is not necessarily a separate franchise.
- Published “Week 4 power rankings” can mean rankings posted after Week 3 for upcoming Week 4; the sheet describes their publication timing.
- Earlier rankings were NOT calculated with the later Option B system unless the saved Week 7–9 documents explicitly say so.
