# Matchup to watch

Pick one game from next week's schedule in each league. It's an editorial choice backed by these factors:

| Factor | Weight (proposed) | Question |
|---|---|---|
| Playoff and standings impact | 35% | Does it move seeding, the playoff line, or first place? |
| Projected competitiveness | 25% | How close is the platform's projected spread? |
| Combined scoring potential | 20% | Are both teams high scorers or contenders? |
| Rivalry and history | 10% | Revenge game, streak on the line, season-long storyline? |
| Storyline and entertainment | 10% | Is this the game the group chat will argue about? |

The weights are a proposal from the methodology audit and haven't been adopted yet. When the pick
goes against the factors because the story is too good, say so in the notes to David.
