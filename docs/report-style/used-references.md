# Used references ledger

David's goal is fresh, current references and dialogue every week, with no recycled bits.
Check this ledger before drafting and add each week's published bits after David signs off.

## Freshness rules

- **Never reuse a tier name.** Every tier label is new each week, in both leagues.
- **Don't reuse a comparison or punchline for at least 4 weeks.** That covers the image itself
  (lottery ticket, National Guard, Geneva Convention), not just the exact wording.
- **Running storylines move forward.** Keep the premise (Tucker's campaign, Lathan's regime) but
  give it a new chapter each week, such as a debate, an indictment, a coup attempt or a recount, based on what happened.
- **Don't share bits across leagues** in the same week. Each league gets its own material.
- **One current hook per report.** Tie at least one joke to something from that week: NFL news, a
  viral moment, pop culture or a real-world headline. Keep it light and not political-partisan.
- **Watch the closers.** "Notify the National Guard" and "the future of democracy" already appear in
  both Week 10 matchups to watch, so retire them for a while.
